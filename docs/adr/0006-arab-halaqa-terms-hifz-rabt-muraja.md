# Use Hifz / Rabt / Muraja'a, not Sabaq / Sabqi / Manzil

The builder prefers the short Arab-world halaqa terms, written in the English alphabet: Hifz (new portion), Rabt (recent revision) and Muraja'a (older revision in rotation). They replace the South Asian sabaq, sabqi and manzil everywhere: UI, code and docs. "Hifz" also appears in the app's name. Inside the app it always means today's new portion, and the overall practice is called "memorisation".
