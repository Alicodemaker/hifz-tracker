# Pace counts the Hifz days actually logged, not every calendar day

The finish estimates ("Ghafir in about 2 weeks, the Quran in about 3 years") multiply the Hifz amount by the number of days a week Hifz was actually logged over the last 4 weeks. Assuming every day would promise dates the builder won't hit, because some days are revision only. Until 4 weeks of history exist, the estimate assumes every day and says so.
