# Muraja'a slices follow surah boundaries, with Juz 30 in two fixed halves

A day's muraja'a is whole surahs in mushaf order, up to 10 pages and never more. The exception is a surah longer than 10 pages, which is split at page boundaries. Juz 30 is always revised as two fixed slices (an-Naba to al-Layl, then ad-Duha to an-Nas), even though the first is about 14 pages. That is how the builder revises it. We rejected a plain "next 10 pages" cut: it would stop mid-surah, and revision in practice happens surah by surah. We also rejected a fixed cycle length, because it makes the daily load grow without the person choosing it.

**Update:** the 10-page limit is now the default of a setting, "Muraja'a per day" (5, 10, 15 or 20 pages), at the builder's request. The rule is unchanged: whole surahs up to the limit, longer surahs split at page boundaries, and Juz 30 in its two fixed halves at any size.
