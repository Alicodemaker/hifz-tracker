# Progress is measured in pages of the 15-line Madani mushaf

All progress, hifz size and revision amounts are measured in pages (with ¼ and ½ fractions) of the 15-line Madani mushaf (604 pages, 20 per juz). Ayahs were too slow to log and surahs too coarse. Page numbers only mean something relative to one printed layout, and stored progress depends on it. Supporting Indo-Pak layouts later would need a page-mapping step, not a setting. Page numbers follow the KFGQPC 1441H edition, the same data the Mushaf page shows (ADR-0007). 56 ayahs at page boundaries sit one page later or earlier than in older printings.
